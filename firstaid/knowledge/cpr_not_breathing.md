# Unresponsive and not breathing (CPR)

Covers: person not breathing, no pulse, cardiac arrest, collapsed and not responding, CPR, using an AED defibrillator.

## Check
1. Check the area is safe.
2. Tap the person's shoulders and shout "Are you okay?".
3. If there is no response, look for normal breathing for no more than 10 seconds. Gasping is not normal breathing.

## What to do
1. Call 108 or 112 immediately, or ask someone else to call. Put the phone on speaker.
2. Ask someone to look for an AED (defibrillator) in the society office or nearby, if one exists.
3. Start chest compressions: kneel beside the person, put the heel of one hand in the centre of the chest, place the other hand on top, keep your arms straight.
4. Push hard and fast: about 5 to 6 cm deep, 100 to 120 times per minute. Let the chest rise fully between pushes.
5. If you are trained, give 2 rescue breaths after every 30 compressions. If you are not trained, keep doing compressions only.
6. Continue until help arrives, an AED is ready, or the person starts breathing normally.
7. If an AED arrives, switch it on and follow its spoken instructions.

## Children and babies
Call for help and start CPR in the same way, using one hand (child) or two fingers (baby), pressing about one third of the chest depth.
