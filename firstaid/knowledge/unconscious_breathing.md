# Unconscious but breathing (recovery position)

Covers: unconscious but breathing, fainted, passed out, not waking up, recovery position, feeling faint.

## What to do
1. Call 108 or 112.
2. Check they are breathing normally. If not, start CPR instead.
3. If there is no suspected spine or neck injury, place them in the recovery position: lay them on their side, top leg bent forward at the knee, head tilted slightly back so the airway stays open and vomit can drain.
4. Loosen tight clothing and keep them warm.
5. Keep checking breathing until help arrives. If breathing stops, roll them on their back and start CPR.

## Fainting
If someone has fainted and is coming round: lay them down and raise their legs a little. Let them rest and get up slowly. Call 108 if they do not wake within a minute, have chest pain, are diabetic, pregnant, or hurt themselves when they fell.

## Do not
Do not give anything to eat or drink. Do not leave them alone.
