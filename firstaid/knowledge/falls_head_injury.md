# Falls, suspected broken bones and head injury

Covers: someone fell or slipped (bathroom, stairs, bed), elderly person fallen and cannot get up, hip or leg pain after a fall, fracture, sprain, hit their head, head wound.

## After a fall
1. Do not rush to lift the person. Ask where it hurts and check if they can move fingers and toes.
2. If they cannot get up, have severe pain, cannot move a limb, or the limb looks bent or out of shape, call 108 or 112.
3. Keep them still and comfortable where they are. Put a pillow under the head only if there is no neck or back pain. Cover them with a blanket to keep warm.
4. Support an injured arm or leg in the position you found it, using pillows or rolled towels. Do not try to straighten it.
5. If they are unhurt and want to get up, help them roll to their side, get onto hands and knees, and rise slowly using a sturdy chair.

## Head injury: call 108 or 112 if
- they were knocked out, even briefly,
- the head is bleeding heavily,
- they are confused, drowsy, vomiting, have a severe headache, blurred vision, or clear fluid from the nose or ears,
- they are elderly or take blood-thinning medicine.
Press a clean cloth on any scalp wound. Do not move them if you suspect a neck or spine injury, unless they are in danger.

## Do not
Do not give food or drink in case they need surgery.
