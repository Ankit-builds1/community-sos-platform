# Poisoning (swallowed chemicals, medicines or cleaners)

Covers: poisoning, drank floor cleaner, bleach, kerosene or pesticide, swallowed too many tablets, child swallowed something harmful.

## What to do
1. Call 108 or 112 immediately, even if the person seems fine at first.
2. Find out what was swallowed, how much and when. Keep the bottle or packet to show the ambulance team.
3. If the person is unconscious but breathing, place them in the recovery position. If not breathing, start CPR, avoiding contact with any chemical around the mouth.
4. If the chemical is on the skin or in the eyes, rinse with plenty of clean running water for at least 15 to 20 minutes.

## Do not
- Do not make them vomit.
- Do not give milk, salt water or any home remedy unless the emergency service tells you to.
