# Fire and gas leaks at home

Covers: fire in the kitchen or flat, smoke, LPG gas cylinder leak, gas smell, short circuit sparks, clothes on fire, trapped by fire.

## Gas leak (LPG smell)
1. Do not switch any electrical switch on or off, do not light a match, and do not use the phone near the leak.
2. Turn off the cylinder regulator and the gas stove knobs if it is safe to reach them.
3. Open all doors and windows.
4. Get everyone out of the flat, then call the gas agency emergency number and 112 from outside.

## Fire
1. Shout to warn everyone and get people out. Your life is more important than belongings.
2. Call 101 (fire) or 112 once you are out.
3. Only fight a fire if it is very small, you have an escape route behind you, and you have the right equipment. Never throw water on an oil or electrical fire. Smother a small pan fire with a lid or damp cloth after turning off the heat.
4. Close doors behind you to slow the fire. Do not use the lift, use the stairs.
5. If there is smoke, stay low near the floor where the air is cleaner, and cover your nose and mouth with a cloth.
6. If your clothes catch fire: stop, drop to the ground, and roll.
7. If you are trapped, close the door, put wet cloth at the bottom gap, go to a window and call for help.
