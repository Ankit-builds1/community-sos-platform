# Severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis)

Covers: allergic reaction, swelling of lips, face or throat, hives, anaphylaxis, bee or wasp sting, food allergy.

## Warning signs
Swelling of the lips, tongue or throat, difficulty breathing or swallowing, wheezing, hoarse voice, widespread rash or hives, dizziness or collapse. It can follow food, insect stings or medicines.

## What to do
1. Call 108 or 112 immediately and say you think it is anaphylaxis.
2. If the person has their own prescribed adrenaline auto-injector, help them use it as they were trained to.
3. If they have breathing difficulty, let them sit up. If they feel faint or dizzy, lay them down with legs raised.
4. Stay with them. If symptoms do not improve and they have a second auto-injector, it may be used as directed on it.
5. If they become unresponsive and are not breathing normally, start CPR.

## Mild reaction or insect sting
For a small local swelling: remove a bee sting by scraping it out sideways, wash the area, and apply a cold compress. Watch for any of the warning signs above.
