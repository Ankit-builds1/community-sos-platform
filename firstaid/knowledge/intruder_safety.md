# Intruder or suspicious person (personal safety)

Covers: someone breaking in, intruder or thief at the door, stranger trying to enter, being followed, suspicious person outside.

## Someone trying to get into your home
1. Do not open the door or confront the person.
2. Lock yourself in a room with a strong door, ideally with your phone.
3. Call 112 (police) and society security. Speak quietly and give your tower and flat number.
4. Stay away from windows and doors. Turn your phone to silent.
5. If the person gets in and you cannot hide, your safety comes first. Do not fight over belongings.

## Being followed
1. Walk towards a busy, well-lit place such as the security gate or a shop.
2. Call security or 112 and stay on the line.
3. Do not go straight to your flat if you are being followed.

## After the incident
Do not touch things the intruder may have handled. Wait for the police.
