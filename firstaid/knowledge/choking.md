# Choking

Covers: choking on food or an object, something stuck in the throat, cannot breathe or speak after eating, baby or child choking.

## Warning signs
Cannot speak, cough or breathe, clutching the throat, lips or face turning blue.

## Adult or child over 1 year
1. If they can cough strongly, encourage them to keep coughing.
2. If they cannot cough, speak or breathe, call 108 or 112 or have someone call.
3. Give up to 5 back blows: lean them forward and hit firmly between the shoulder blades with the heel of your hand.
4. If that does not work, give up to 5 abdominal thrusts: stand behind them, put a fist just above the belly button, grab it with your other hand and pull sharply inwards and upwards.
5. Repeat 5 back blows and 5 abdominal thrusts until the object comes out or help arrives.
6. If they become unresponsive, start CPR.

## Baby under 1 year
1. Hold the baby face down along your forearm, supporting the head, and give up to 5 back blows.
2. Turn the baby face up and give up to 5 chest thrusts with two fingers in the centre of the chest.
3. Do not use abdominal thrusts on a baby. Call 108 or 112.

## Do not
Do not put your fingers in the mouth to search blindly for the object.
