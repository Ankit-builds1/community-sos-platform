# Stroke

Covers: stroke, face drooping, slurred speech, sudden weakness or numbness on one side, sudden confusion, paralysis.

## Warning signs (FAST)
- Face: one side of the face drooping, uneven smile.
- Arms: cannot lift both arms and keep them up, or one arm is weak or numb.
- Speech: slurred, confused or unable to speak.
- Time: call 108 or 112 immediately. Every minute matters.
Other signs: sudden confusion, sudden loss of vision, sudden severe headache, loss of balance.

## What to do
1. Call 108 or 112 and say you think it is a stroke.
2. Note the exact time the symptoms started. Doctors need this.
3. Keep them comfortable and lying or sitting with head and shoulders slightly raised.
4. If they become unconscious but are breathing, use the recovery position. If not breathing, start CPR.

## Do not
Do not give food, drink or any medicine, because swallowing may be affected.
