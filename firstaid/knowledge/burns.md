# Burns and scalds

Covers: burn, scald from hot water or oil, electric shock burn, chemical burn, blisters on skin.

## What to do
1. Stop the burning: move away from the heat source and switch off electricity if it is an electrical burn (do not touch the person until the power is off).
2. Cool the burn under cool or lukewarm running water for 20 minutes. Do not use ice.
3. Remove rings, watches or clothing near the burn, unless they are stuck to the skin.
4. Cover loosely with cling film or a clean plastic bag, or a clean non-fluffy cloth.
5. Keep the person warm (cool the burn, not the whole body).

## Call 108 or 112 if
- the burn is larger than the person's palm, deep, or charred or white,
- it is on the face, hands, feet, groin or a joint,
- it is an electrical or chemical burn,
- the person is a child, elderly, or has breathing difficulty from smoke.

## Do not
Do not burst blisters. Do not apply butter, toothpaste, oil or creams.
