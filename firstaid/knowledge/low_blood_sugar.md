# Low blood sugar (diabetic emergency)

Covers: low sugar, hypoglycemia, diabetic person shaky, sweating, dizzy or confused, sugar level dropped.

## Warning signs
Shaking, sweating, pale skin, hunger, dizziness, confusion, unusual behaviour, irritability, drowsiness. It can come on quickly in people with diabetes, especially after missing a meal or after exercise.

## What to do if they are awake and can swallow
1. Give something sugary straight away: fruit juice, a regular (non-diet) soft drink, glucose tablets, sugar or sweets.
2. Let them rest and sit down.
3. If they feel better after 10 to 15 minutes, give a snack like biscuits or bread.
4. If they do not improve, give more sugar and call 108 or 112.

## If they are drowsy, confused or unconscious
1. Call 108 or 112.
2. Do not give anything by mouth.
3. If unconscious but breathing, place them in the recovery position. If not breathing, start CPR.
