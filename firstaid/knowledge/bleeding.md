# Severe bleeding and cuts

Covers: bleeding that will not stop, deep cut, knife cut while cooking, wound, glass cut, nosebleed with heavy blood.

## What to do
1. If possible, wear gloves or put a plastic bag over your hand.
2. Press firmly and directly on the wound with a clean cloth, towel or dressing.
3. Keep pressing without stopping for at least 10 minutes. If blood soaks through, add more cloth on top, do not remove the first one.
4. If the wound is on an arm or leg and there is no broken bone, raise it above the level of the heart while pressing.
5. Call 108 or 112 if the bleeding is heavy, spurting, does not stop after 10 minutes of pressure, or the wound is deep or large.
6. Keep the person lying down and warm if they feel faint, pale or cold.

## Object stuck in the wound
Do not pull it out. Press around it and call 108.

## Small cuts
Rinse with clean water, press until bleeding stops, cover with a clean plaster.
