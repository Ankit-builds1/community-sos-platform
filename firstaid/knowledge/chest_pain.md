# Chest pain (possible heart attack)

Covers: chest pain, chest tightness or pressure, heart attack, pain spreading to the arm or jaw, sweating with chest pain.

## Warning signs
Pain, pressure or tightness in the centre of the chest lasting more than a few minutes, or that comes and goes. It may spread to the left arm, jaw, neck or back. Other signs include sweating, breathlessness, nausea, dizziness and a feeling of doom. Women, diabetics and elderly people may have milder or unusual symptoms.

## What to do
1. Call 108 (ambulance) or 112 immediately. Do not wait to see if it passes.
2. Help the person sit down and rest in a half-sitting position, leaning against a wall or pillows, with knees bent. Do not let them walk around.
3. Loosen tight clothing at the neck, chest and waist.
4. If they have their own prescribed heart medicine and have been told by a doctor to take it for chest pain, help them take it as prescribed.
5. Stay with them, keep them calm, and note the time the pain started.
6. If they become unresponsive and are not breathing normally, start CPR (see the CPR guide).

## Do not
- Do not give food or drink.
- Do not drive them yourself if an ambulance can come.
