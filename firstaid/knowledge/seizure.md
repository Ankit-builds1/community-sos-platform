# Seizure (fits)

Covers: seizure, fits, convulsions, shaking uncontrollably, epilepsy attack.

## What to do
1. Stay calm and note the time the seizure starts.
2. Move hard or sharp objects away. Put something soft under their head.
3. Loosen anything tight around the neck.
4. Do not hold them down or try to stop the movements.
5. When the shaking stops, turn them on their side in the recovery position and check breathing.
6. Stay with them until they are fully awake. Speak calmly.

## Call 108 or 112 if
- the seizure lasts more than 5 minutes, or a second one starts,
- they do not wake up or have trouble breathing afterwards,
- it is their first seizure, they are injured, pregnant, diabetic, or it happened in water.

## Do not
Do not put anything in their mouth. Do not give food, drink or medicine until fully awake.
